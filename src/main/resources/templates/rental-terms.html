Before you book

Rental agreement summary.

This is a project demo template. A real rental business must replace it with its reviewed, jurisdiction-specific legal terms.

1. Driver eligibility

Customers must hold a valid licence appropriate for the vehicle and present accepted identity documents. Staff should verify documents before handing over the bike.

2. Pickup and return

Pick up and return at the agreed location and time. Late returns may incur additional hourly charges. Contact support as soon as possible if your plans change.

3. Pricing and payment

Prices displayed before booking are estimates based on the selected duration, add-ons, applicable promotions and pricing rules. Security deposits, taxes and payment handling must be clearly disclosed by the operator.

4. Condition and damage

Customer and staff should record the bike condition at pickup and return, including photographs where appropriate. Damage and deposit decisions must be reviewed fairly and documented.

5. Cancellation and refunds

Upcoming bookings can be cancelled from the customer dashboard in this demo. A real operator must define cancellation windows, refund eligibility and processing times.

6. Safety and breakdowns

Follow road laws, use suitable protective equipment and stop in a safe location if a breakdown occurs. Real emergency assistance contact details must be supplied by the rental operator.

7. Privacy

Personal information should only be collected for a clear purpose, protected against unauthorised access, and retained only as long as necessary. Do not use demo forms to submit full identity-document numbers.

By accepting the terms in the booking flow, you confirm that you have read this demo summary. It is not a substitute for legal review.
